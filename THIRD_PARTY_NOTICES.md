## Embedded fonts

`client.js` embeds the Latin and Latin Extended subsets of two variable fonts
as data URLs (generated by `build-fonts.mjs` from the `@fontsource-variable`
packages). Both are licensed under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/).

## Fonts referenced but not shipped

Font stacks also name fonts such as HarmonyOS Sans SC, Fira Code, JetBrains
Mono, Cascadia Code, Victor Mono, Source Code Pro, Mona Sans and Monaspace Neon.
Hanmo does not include them; a font that is not installed is skipped and
the next one in the stack is used.
